# HOW TO ADD YOUR ACTUAL CERTIFICATES
1. Place your certificate files (PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP) directly in this folder:
   e.g.:
   - nri-u-hackathon.pdf
   - mern-stack-internship.pdf
   - ibm-aiml.pdf
   - aws-data-engineering.pdf
   - infosys-python.pdf
   - cisco-cybersecurity.pdf

2. Open `src/data/certificates.js` and set the `file` field for each item:
   e.g.:
   file: "/certificates/nri-u-hackathon.pdf"

3. That's it! The Certificate Vault and Document Viewer will immediately display your certificates with preview, zoom, fullscreen, and download capabilities.
